Starting a U.S. Business

Can You Form a Company Without Living in America?

Yes. Citizens and residents of Uzbekistan can own a U.S. LLC without U.S. citizenship, a Green Card, or U.S. residency. You can work with international clients, sign contracts, and manage your company remotely from Uzbekistan. Forming a company does not provide U.S. immigration or work authorization.

Financial Services

Can You Open a U.S. Business Bank Account?

With your LLC and federal employer identification number — EIN — you may apply for a business account with eligible banks or financial technology providers.

Banks may request formation documents, identity verification, an actual operating address, and business details. Some may require an in-person visit.

Account approval is solely the financial institution’s decision. We do not guarantee bank approval; we can assist with preparation of company documents.

EIN — Federal Tax Identification Number

We assist with submitting your EIN application to the IRS. The IRS issues EINs at no charge; our package includes professional application assistance.

Obtaining an EIN does not automatically qualify you for bank or marketplace accounts.
