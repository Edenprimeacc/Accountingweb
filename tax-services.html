Our Tax Approach

A Complete Review Before Every Filing.

At Eden Prime Accounting, tax preparation is only one part of what we do. We provide consulting, planning, and ongoing support through a full-service, integrated approach.

Before preparing a return, we review the company’s legal structure, ownership, business activity, state of operation, and applicable filing requirements. This helps reduce the risk of returns being filed under an incorrect entity classification or without considering important compliance obligations.

We also review the bookkeeping records supporting the tax return. When requested, we provide cleanup and organization services so the accounting records can support reliable financial statements, tax preparation, and audit readiness.

Deductions and filing requirements vary by entity type, industry, state, ownership structure, and business activity. We evaluate the client’s full situation to identify legitimate deductions and applicable filing obligations.

Prepared beyond the filing deadline Your records are organized not only for the current return, but also for financial reporting, lender requests, future planning, and possible federal or state review.
