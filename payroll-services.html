More Than Paychecks

Payroll Built Around Your Business.

Payroll is more than issuing employee paychecks. At Eden Prime Accounting, we provide complete payroll management solutions designed to help businesses pay employees accurately, meet federal and state obligations, and simplify payroll administration.

We review employee and contractor information, payroll schedules, compensation details, tax withholding, reporting requirements, and year-end forms. Our goal is to support accurate payroll processing while helping businesses reduce errors, missed deadlines, and unnecessary penalties.

Whether you have one employee or a growing team, our payroll services can be tailored to your company’s size, payment schedule, state registrations, and reporting responsibilities.

Year-round payroll support Our services include payroll processing, tax filings, W-2 and 1099 reporting, payroll cleanup, notices, and compliance support throughout the year.

W-2 & 1099 Reporting

Employee and Contractor Year-End Forms.

Year-end payroll and information reporting are critical responsibilities for every employer and business. Eden Prime Accounting prepares and files required employee and contractor forms accurately and on time.

We help businesses review payroll records, verify recipient information, prepare required forms, support electronic filing, and organize copies for employees, contractors, and company records.

Employee Forms

W-2 & W-3

  • Wage and tax statement preparation
  • Federal and state reporting review
  • W-3 transmittal filing
  • Employee copy distribution
  • Correction support when needed
Contractor Forms

1099-NEC & 1099-MISC

  • Contractor payment reporting
  • Recipient information review
  • Electronic filing support
  • Recipient copy distribution
  • IRS and state reporting review
Our Payroll Process

A Clear Process From Setup to Filing.

Our payroll workflow helps keep employee payments, tax calculations, filings, and reports organized throughout each payroll period.

STEP 01

Information Review

We review employee, contractor, pay rate, deduction, and payroll schedule information.

STEP 02

Payroll Calculation

Gross wages, withholding, deductions, employer taxes, and net pay are calculated.

STEP 03

Payroll Approval

Payroll details are reviewed before employee payments are finalized.

STEP 04

Employee Payments

Direct deposit or payroll payment information is processed according to the payroll schedule.

STEP 05

Tax Filing

Applicable federal and state payroll returns and payment obligations are addressed.

STEP 06

Reports & Year-End Forms

Payroll reports, W-2 forms, W-3, and applicable 1099 forms are prepared and organized.
